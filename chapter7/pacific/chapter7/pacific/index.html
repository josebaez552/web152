Enjoy Nature in Luxury

Pacific Trails Resort is a rustic retreat in the majestic redwoods of California's North Coast. Our resort is located on the edge of the continent, where the mountains meet the sea. We offer a variety of accommodations, including cozy yurts and comfortable lodge rooms. Our guests enjoy hiking, kayaking, and relaxing on the beach.

  • Unwind in the heated outdoor pool and whirlpools
  • Explore the coast on your own or join our guided tours
  • Relax in our lodge while enjoying complimentary appetizers and beverages
  • Savor nightly fine dining with an ocean view
