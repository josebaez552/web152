What is a Yurt?

Our luxury yurts are permanent structures four feet off the ground. Each yurt is fully enclosed with canvas walls, a wooden floor, and a roof dome that can be opened.

Activities at Pacific Trails

Hiking

Pacific Trails Resort has 5 miles of scenic hiking trails for all skill levels.

Beachcombing

Our resort is located on the edge of the continent, where the mountains meet the sea. Enjoy a walk along the beach and discover the wonders of the tide pools.

Bird Watching

Bring your binoculars and enjoy the diverse wildlife that calls Pacific Trails home.
